About

What Oh the Places I've Been is, and what it does.

Oh the Places I've Been is a free travel journal for keeping track of the places you have visited. It started as a simple idea: a map you can look at and say, "I have been to all of those." Over time it grew into a small set of tools for remembering and sharing your travels.

What the app does

  • A world map of your visits. Add a place and it appears as a pin. Countries and US states you have visited are highlighted.
  • Photos, ratings and notes. Attach pictures, a star rating and a few notes to each place, and tap a photo to view it full screen.
  • A trip timeline. A scrollable timeline of your trips by date, with year markers.
  • Travel stats. Countries, US states and continents visited, and the percentage of the world you have covered.
  • Share cards. Turn a trip, a year, or your whole travel profile into a photo-collage image to share.
  • Friends and feed. Follow other travelers, see their trips, and like and comment on places they have been.
  • Recommendations. Suggestions for where to go next, based on the places you have rated highly.

Where to use it

You can use the app in your web browser right now, and there is an Android version as well. It is free to use and is supported by advertising.

About the guides

The Travel Journal section collects practical guides on topics that come up when you track your travels: how to keep a journal, how countries and states are counted, how to organize photos, how to rate the places you have visited, and how to plan the next trip. They are general-interest guides for travelers.
