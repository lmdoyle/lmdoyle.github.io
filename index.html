Oh the Places I've Been

A free travel journal. Pin the places you've visited on a world map, keep your photos and ratings together, and see how much of the world you've covered.

Open the app

What you can do

Map your travels

Add each city you've visited and see it as a pin on a world map, with the countries and US states you've been to highlighted.

Trip timeline

Scroll a timeline of your trips by date, with year markers so you can see when you went where.

Photos and ratings

Attach photos, a star rating and notes to every place, and tap any photo to view it full screen.

Travel stats

Track countries, US states and continents visited, and what percentage of the world you've seen.

Share your trips

Turn a trip, a whole year, or your travel profile into a photo-collage card you can share on social media.

Follow friends

Follow other travelers, see their trips in your feed, and like and comment on places they've been.

Ideas for your next trip

Get suggestions for where to go next, based on the places you've rated highly.

Free to use

Oh the Places I've Been is free and supported by ads. You can use it in your browser right now, or get it on Android.
